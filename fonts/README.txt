Put TrueType/OpenType fonts (.ttf, .otf, .ttc) in this folder to use them in the game.

In Options > Graphics, set "Font Style" to "Smooth", then pick the font in the "Font" field
below it. The fonts are drawn in the size and colors of the graphics pack's pixel fonts.
Fonts with accented letters are needed to show the Spanish and French texts with their accents.
